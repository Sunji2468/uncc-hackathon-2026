From plate to plan to community

Snap it

Photograph a restaurant plate, or the odds and ends on your counter.

Flip it

Each photo becomes a sticker. Flip it for estimated macros, a recipe and the price gap.

Cook it

Set the time dial and the serving slider. The recipe rewrites itself to fit.

Track it

Drag stickers into today. The bars fill, and the app suggests a meal sized to what is left.

Share it

Post it to the Community Fridge. Friends drop reaction stickers and sticky notes right on your dish.

Estimates, with the error bars showing

Calories and macros read from a photo are educated guesses, because portion size and hidden oil are hard to see. TasteHeist labels them as estimates and lets you correct the portion.
